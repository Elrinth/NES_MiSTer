Mapper 682 services revision

Fixes the reproduced CPU Cycle IRQ -> PPU Scanline IRQ mainline freeze by
acknowledging scanline IRQ on NMI vector fetches, matching Mesen. The old
mapper fails the targeted T65 regression; this version passes. CPU Cycle
IRQ's displayed value 5 is expected. Hardware confirmation is still needed.

Adds sprite extended banks, original OAM-index tracking, OAM clear and
extended-bank update routines, and NMI/IRQ vector redirection. Includes
save-state coverage for the new state. Use Extra Sprites Off to compare
with the ordinary NES sprite limit in Mesen.

PRG mapping is unchanged. Both PRG diagnostic ROM variants passed on the
user's testfix core. Original-ROM cold-start PRG results remain to confirm.
No claim of complete Rainbow compatibility: Wi-Fi/ESP, self-flashing,
IPCM and EPSM remain unsupported.

See source/tests/mapper682/README.md and round3_* logs for verification.
The *_before_result log deliberately records the previous core's failure.
